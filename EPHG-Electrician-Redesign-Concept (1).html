    The basics of how a call-out works.

    What happens when I call?

    Tell the team what's happened. They'll talk through the issue, coverage and booking terms with you before arranging a call-out.

    Do I need an electrician or a heating engineer?

    For boiler or heating problems, EPHG will confirm whether an electrician or a Gas Safe heating engineer is the right person to send.

    Is there a call-out deposit?

    A call-out deposit and the applicable rates should be confirmed before a booking is finalised. Full details are in the terms.

    Where do you cover?

    EPHG provides emergency electrical, plumbing, heating and gas call-out services across much of the UK. Call and check your postcode.

    What should I do while I wait?

    Use the quick guide above. If there is a fire or someone is hurt, call 999. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999.
